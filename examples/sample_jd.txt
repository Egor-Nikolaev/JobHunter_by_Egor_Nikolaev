Product Manager (AI / Automation) — B2B SaaS platform

About the role:
We are looking for a Product Manager to own product strategy and roadmap for our
B2B SaaS platform. You will run discovery and user research, define hypotheses,
and validate them with A/B experiments. You will prioritize the backlog (RICE),
track product metrics (retention, conversion, activation), and drive go-to-market.

Responsibilities:
- Own product vision and roadmap; align stakeholders across engineering and design
- Lead discovery: customer interviews, JTBD, opportunity assessment
- Define and monitor KPIs and unit economics
- Integrate LLM automation into product workflows (Claude, n8n, REST API)
- Coordinate cross-functional delivery in Agile / Scrum; manage release and risk

Requirements:
- 4+ years in product management, ideally FinTech or enterprise SaaS
- Strong analytics; comfortable with SQL and dashboards
- Experience with prompt engineering and AI orchestration is a plus
- Tools: Jira, Confluence, Notion, Figma
- English B2+; remote-friendly
