How it works

Six stages. One command each.

It mirrors how a sharp operator actually runs a search — just without the copy-paste, the tab chaos, and the forgotten follow-ups.

01

Onboard

It interviews you and builds your profile.

02

Master résumé

Your career, structured once.

03

Tailor

04

Reach out

Personalized message per recruiter.

05

Follow up

Chases silence, fills forms.

06

Track

Every application on one board.

What's inside

Built for volume, tuned for signal.

The whole pipeline is scripted and config-driven. You bring the keys; it brings the throughput.

🎯

Per-vacancy tailoring

Matches each job description against your skill bank, rewrites the headline and skills line, and compiles a clean PDF. Filename equals ad name, so every stat stays traceable.

📡

Multi-channel outreach

Telegram job channels, hh.ru, Hirify — a distinct, personalized message per recruiter with your résumé attached. LinkedIn-ready.

↩️

Follow-ups that don't forget

No reply in N days? A polite nudge goes out automatically. The tail of a funnel is where most people quietly lose interviews.

📝

Form auto-fill

Application form in the way? It fills your details from your profile and — if you let it — submits.

📊

Command deck

A live dashboard: sent, replied, interviews, by source. See what needs you at a glance.

🌍

Bring your own proxy

Region-locked board? Plug in your own proxy key for access. Nothing hardcoded, nothing shared.

Your data stays yours

Zero secrets in the box.

The repo ships with a fictional persona and placeholder keys only. You fill your own config locally — it's git-ignored by default. No accounts, no telemetry, no data leaving your machine.
